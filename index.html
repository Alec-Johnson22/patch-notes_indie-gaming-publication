-Short Note Regarding S1-

-I chose box-sizing: border-box as my main reset rule. I intentionally left margin and padding blank so as to not add any addition spacing than what was intended.

-Two important decisions I made for this site were the spacing of cards and my color palette. I wanted the screen to be filled with different articles. I also chose the darker
background as many people that are chronically online, i.e. gamers, tend to use dark mode. I figured I could lean into that design and provide dark mode as the default for the page.
